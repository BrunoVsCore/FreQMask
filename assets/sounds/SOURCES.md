# Fontes dos sons

Os ruidos coloridos sao gerados localmente pelo app para manter qualidade limpa e loop infinito.

Sons ambientes baixados do Mixkit, sob Mixkit Sound Effects Free License:

- `rain.mp3`: Long rain ambience
- `fan.mp3`: Small stove extractor fan
- `air.mp3`: Refrigerator hum
- `travel.mp3`: Old train departure
- `ocean.mp3`: Sea coast breaking waves
- `fireplace.mp3`: Campfire night wind
- `wind.mp3`: Wind blowing ambience
- `stream.mp3`: River water flowing
- `city.mp3`: City traffic background ambience

Fonte: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
